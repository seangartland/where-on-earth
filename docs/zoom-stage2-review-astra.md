# Stage 2 review and renderer-pilot decision

2026-11-21. Design review only. Recommendation: **proceed with the stated changes**, conditionally through one bounded asset revision before implementing the renderer. Do not proceed with the unchanged 4x4 residency plan. Do not expand the world inventory. If the revision cannot close the asset and footprint gates below, pivot to direct final-z9 persistent GPU tiles for evaluation under the same gates.

This memo uses the [original design](zoom-diagnosis-astra.md), [reconsideration](zoom-reconsideration-astra.md), [pilot report](zoom-stage2-evidence/stage2-report.md), [manifest](zoom-stage2-evidence/pilot-manifest.jsonl), and the two supplied JPEGs. Measurements attributed to the Pi are supplied evidence, not new access to that machine. Local checks parsed all manifest rows, summed their bytes and source counts, and checked the two local JPEG sizes. I visually inspected both JPEGs. No code, source assets, deployment, or commits were changed.

## 1. Exit conditions

**Verdict: asset size and numeric integrity pass their narrow checks; visual/source fidelity and gutters remain marginal; the runtime manifest contract and unchanged residency plan fail. Stage 2 has not earned unconditional renderer entry.**

| Condition | Verdict | Evidence and remaining requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Encoded size | Pass as pilot evidence, marginal for complete preparation | Measured 142 pages, 15,879,897 bytes; median 112,030 bytes. 39/41 measured footprints fit 3,145,728 bytes. The two Madrid natural windows each exceed it by exactly 7,042 bytes. Do not round that overage away. |
| Visual and source fidelity | Marginal, not certified | Tokyo looks geographically coherent with no obvious internal grid seams, consistent with the orchestrator's observation. Madrid likewise shows no obvious internal grid seams at this inspection scale. Neither inspection is a matched original-source comparison or a phone minification test. Validate JPEG quality, fallback boundaries, color/style and intended source fidelity at fixed poses before renderer entry. |
| Gutters | Marginal, unresolved | The report identifies nine pages with gutter fallback. Fallback is legitimate only if it reproduces the intended neighboring resolved mosaic. A recorded substitute is not proof of continuity. Check every flagged page against its neighbors, including wrap and coarse-level edges; then check cross-page sampling at allowed scales. Two isolated images cannot certify external seams or independent mip chains. |
| Numeric manifest integrity | Pass within scope | Local sum matches 15,879,897 bytes, 142 unique page rows; both supplied JPEG sizes match. Accept the supplied independent Pi file-size reconciliation, 15 readbacks and three byte-identical rebuilds. These do not amount to a local audit of all 142 image files. |
| Runtime manifest correctness | Fail as delivered contract | Rows contain key, absolute Pi path, bytes, dimensions, bounds, existence, sites and 16 source levels. They contain no content version, content hash, runtime URL, or per-page gutter fallback record. All rows have `exists: true`; this positive inventory does not define absent versus outside-pilot coverage. Complete the versioned bounded manifest before use; reject unknown keys outside its declared domain rather than assuming absence. |
| Complete footprint residency | Fail unchanged | Only 23/41 fit both original budgets. 18/41 exceed the 11-texture capacity; the maximum is 16. This is a deterministic workload failure, not a phone memory measurement or a population failure rate. The original nine-z9-page selection limit is an additional restriction; `fits-11` alone does not prove full compliance with it. |
| Operational counters | Fail reconciliation | The reported zero host fetches contradict the disclosed 2,654 fetched cache tiles. Discard that counter section for input reads, traffic, errors and cost inference. Repair and reconcile counters before any scaling. Output bytes and separately validated asset results stand. |

Measured provenance totals agree with the brief: 2,272 cells comprising 829 z9, 271 z8, 612 z7, 548 z5 and 12 base-equivalent cells. Seven pages contain base-equivalent cells. The supplied holes result has 56/70 z9 cells missing. These are valid reasons for declared fallback, not permission to downgrade pixels where native source exists. The counts mix page zoom levels and overlapping geographic extents; they are not the phone's native-visible-area percentage. Cell source levels must be supported by source identity/version and evidence that each whole cell resolves as declared. The report alone cannot prove that a partially missing input is represented faithfully.

There is a brief/report discrepancy worth preserving: recomputing nearest-rank p95 from the manifest gives **262,654 bytes at z9**, as in the report, rather than the brief's 247,956. Overall p95 is 260,364; z9 median is 98,151.5. This does not alter any footprint sum or verdict. The 1.82 GiB median-based and 4.24 GiB p95-based full-inventory figures remain **estimates**, not measured storage forecasts or confidence bounds. No representative terrain-weighted global mean has been established.

## 2. Residency ruling and arithmetic

**Verdict: retain 64 MiB, RGBA8, full mip chains and two-pixel gutters. Change z9 page geometry to 2x2 tiles for one bounded recut; retain 4x4 z7/z5 context. Replace geometry-dependent page-count limits with byte accounting and a bounded 40-texture safety ceiling for this experiment.** This explicitly supersedes the nine-target-page and twelve-total-page limits, not any latency or byte gate.

The 64 MiB limit is an engineering allocation limit for page textures, not a discovered Safari memory ceiling. The original design calculated roughly 85.3 MiB for the two legacy portrait buffers including mips, before base, framebuffer, browser and decode allocations. Replacing them with a 64 MiB bounded store leaves some relative headroom. It does not prove safety on Sean's unspecified iPhone or an older supported phone. Record those devices and thermal behavior and disable legacy inset allocation in the page arm. Track base/framebuffer memory, live decoded images, pending uploads and disposal separately. If phone evidence requires a lower limit, lower it and re-run fit; there is no evidence here to raise it.

The following are **calculated texture-payload estimates**, excluding driver padding, backing copies and other renderer allocations. For an RGBA8 square, sum `4 * floor(N / 2^level)^2` through the 1x1 mip. Encoded JPEG bytes do not predict this allocation.

| Layout | Estimated bytes per full mip chain | Estimated MiB |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1028 square, existing 4x4 plus gutters | 5,635,496 | 5.37443 |
| 1024 square, without added gutter extent | 5,592,404 | 5.33333 |
| 516 square, proposed 2x2 plus gutters | 1,419,688 | 1.35392 |
| 258 square, illustrative single tile plus one-pixel sides | 354,664 | 0.33823 |

Eleven original pages estimate to 59.12 MiB, twelve to 64.49 MiB and sixteen to 85.99 MiB. The report's 59.18/64.56/86.08 values multiply its rounded 5.38 estimate; either method yields the same pass/fail decisions. Twelve 1024-square full chains nearly consume all 64 MiB, leaving no context headroom and losing the specified neighbor gutters. The 1028 dimension is not the fundamental problem.

Ranked levers, considering residency gain against implementation and quality damage:

1. **Stop retaining context that contributes no pixels at the current scale.** Cull fully occluded context and let z9 mips cover their own geography. Each original context page avoided saves an estimated 5.374 MiB without reducing final source fidelity. However, surrounding geography and zoom reversal still require context. Great Lakes' site inventory contains twelve z9 and four context pages: even removing all four leaves an estimated 64.49 MiB. Context scheduling is worthwhile but cannot alone rescue the unchanged grid. Do not assume all context is removable during the full gesture.
2. **Reduce fixed-grid alignment waste with smaller z9 pages.** This is the decisive quality-preserving lever. An integer-aligned 7x10 tile window touches at most 4x6 = 24 two-tile pages, versus up to twelve four-tile pages. That is 96 versus 192 allocated tile interiors for 70 wanted cells. Estimated z9 allocation falls from 64.49 to 32.49 MiB at those maxima. Four unchanged context pages add 21.50 MiB, giving an estimated 53.99 MiB total. Even a conservative eight context pages, four per coarse level, total an estimated 75.48 MiB, so the actual union must still be measured. The pilot's worst site unions have at most four context pages; verify exact camera coverage rather than generalizing that property globally. Cost: more requests, textures and draws, new packing, and fresh seam tests. The target maximum rises to 24 smaller pages for these windows, not 24 original pages.
3. **Change mip allocation only if later measurements justify it.** Removing all original-page mips reduces estimated per-page allocation to 4.03131 MiB, saving about 25%. Sixteen base levels still need 64.50 MiB. It also undermines intermediate-scale sampling and may add shimmering or ladder dependencies. Limited chains save less. Keep mips in this experiment; do not trade away the reason prepared final detail can render through a zoom.
4. **Change format or resolution.** JPEG opacity does not guarantee three-byte GPU storage. RGB accounting must not assume a mobile driver saves 25%. Block compression could buy substantial residency but adds transcoding, capability, quality and upload risks not measured here. Downsampling context to 514-square mipmapped textures would estimate roughly 1.34 MiB each, but changes intermediate-view detail and gutter handling. Neither is the first bounded change; any future tier must match intended quality at its actual projected scale and may never replace terminal z9 pixels for scoring.
5. **Raise the memory cap.** Accommodating sixteen unchanged textures would require about 86 MiB before overhead. This spends device headroom without fixing overfetch or the 3 MiB failures. Reject it.

Build the combination of smaller z9 pages plus context culling, with full-quality context budgeted conservatively until occlusion is proved. Recut from the original approved source mosaic, not by recompressing the supplied JPEGs. Two-pixel gutters become 516-square assets with four-cell provenance. Estimated geometric savings are credible grounds for one bounded asset revision, not a claim that revised transfer or five-second preparation already passes. Smaller pages may increase JPEG overhead and request latency. Measure every revised footprint's bytes and all simultaneously visible, pinned and context allocations before renderer entry. The two Madrid cases must be remeasured, not assumed cured.

## 3. Exact over-cap behavior

**Verdict: explicit partial preparation with stable resident detail and declared coarse fallback; no gesture-time eviction of overlapping visible pages to rotate detail through an impossible full view.** A lower-quality perimeter is usable fallback, but it is not a successful Tsharp sample where higher-quality intended pixels exist.

Implement the following contract for either page size:

1. Compute the final target view plus its one-z9-tile margin and the required context for the preparation path. Use a versioned manifest and actual viewport/orientation. Union keys with live visible pages, deduplicate, and reserve exact estimated allocation bytes, including mip levels and any replacement overlap, before dispatch. Use actual encoded lengths for transfer reservations. Record the full required set separately from the selected subset.
2. A full plan fits only if its residency union is within 64 MiB, its texture count within the revised ceiling, and its speculative transfer within 3 MiB. Already resident resources cost no new transfer but still cost residency. Count abandoned speculative transfers against the same round allowance; cache hits do not replenish already spent bytes. Save Data permits zero speculative transfer.
3. If it cannot fit, mark `partial` with the limiting resource and missing keys. Preserve currently visible resident pages first. Select target pages by center-containing page, then increasing distance to target, then stable key order, reserving necessary fallback context as part of the same budget. Skip a candidate that cannot fit and continue to smaller eligible candidates. This selects a geographic subset, not a secretly smaller scoring viewport. Evict only nonvisible, unpinned LRU resources to make room. Release farthest target-only pins when live visibility needs the space.
4. At input, keep all overlapping visible pages. Admit new visible detail only within freed or unallocated capacity; never evict a still-visible page just to cycle another part of the same impossible view. Use the nearest approved resident ancestor or ready base immediately for uncovered regions. For a fixed final pose whose complete detail cannot fit, leave it explicitly incomplete and record a residency failure rather than spinning uploads. On pan, evict pages that leave visibility as needed; retain every overlapping resident page. Never restart healthy neighbors when one request fails.
5. `Complete` requires the entire selected full plan, including margin and required context, GPU-ready before the gesture and no remaining dependency-linked fetch, decode or upload. Partial, evicted and context-lost states cannot be relabeled complete because the center looks sharp. A changed viewport or target invalidates the certificate and triggers a new plan. Context restoration loses GPU preparation even if compressed bodies survive.

Tests must cover original 12/13/15/16-page cases, both Madrid byte failures, the exact-cap boundary, current visible pages competing with target pages, orientation change, round cancellation, missing assets, and context restoration. Assert allocation reservations never overshoot, overlapping pages survive one-tile pans, and a fixed over-cap pose cannot cause eviction churn. Score every over-cap five-second-dwell round in the whole-experience denominator. The 18/41 failures cannot simply be excluded; if they remain unable to attain intended detail, this workload cannot satisfy the unchanged whole-experience gate. Partial behavior is a failure-safe rendering policy, not the proposed route to acceptance.

## 4. Build order, phone measurements and stop rules

**Verdict: proceed with the stated changes only through the following staged exits.** This memo authorizes no implementation in this review task. It recommends a later bounded experiment, not production rollout.

1. **Close asset evidence first.** Repair the version/hash/URL/absence contract and counter reconciliation. Compare both supplied pages and all flagged fallback/gutter cases against resolved originals, validate neighboring edges, wrap, source identity and hashes. Recut only the same proof areas at 2x2 z9, retain coarse assets, and replay all 41 windows plus true phone portrait/landscape bounds and transition context. Publish exact selected key sets, bytes, texture estimates and overfetch. All 41 bounded reference windows must admit complete plans within the unchanged byte caps before proceeding with this revision. This is a deliberately conservative asset exit, not a new population latency claim. If source quality, gutters or this fit test fails, stop page expansion and review the persistent tile fallback. Do not run an open-ended series of page geometries.
2. **Repair instrumentation and establish the phone baseline.** Capture model, iOS/Safari version, DPR, viewport, refresh rate and thermal/battery state on Sean's iPhone. Log gesture and round IDs, final pose, per-frame intended-source coverage, unique requests, cache tier, transfer bytes, upload dependencies, actual allocation dimensions/formats/levels and disposal. Export each round before ring wrap. Distinguish GPU completion from CPU upload submission; use presented-frame evidence and supported GPU timing without blocking the timed path. Uncompleted sharpness is a failure or explicitly bounded censored observation, never zero latency.
3. **Implement the bounded persistent store and renderer.** Use shared versioned requests, four concurrent fetches with at most two target-only, two decodes and at most one upload per frame. Preserve visible priority and the partial contract above. Disable legacy inset buffers in this arm. First measure individual page decode/upload and mip cost, then maximum-footprint residency, seams and scale transitions on the phone. Include allocation during replacement and disposal, not just steady-state texture totals. A single upload-attributable frame above 50 ms or any cap violation stops advancement for correction and retest.
4. **Connect current-round preparation and run the paired experiment.** Start with Great Lakes shifted, Madrid natural, Tokyo holes and high-latitude tall windows, since easy fits cannot test the disputed design. Then run every original proof site: Tokyo, Madrid, boundary, Congo, Great Lakes, atoll, ordinary land without z10, holes, ocean, antimeridian and high latitude. Include portrait/landscape, home-spin-pinch and direct deep jump, reversal, small and off-target pans, revisit, abandonment, round changes during loading, background/foreground and context restoration. Keep current behavior as the baseline and Arm B's rejection documented; any repaired cheaper comparison that passes every gate ends page expansion for review.
5. **Validate distributions and sustained behavior.** Use 30 interleaved paired repetitions per principal site/profile and at least five functional repetitions per boundary case. Profile A is measured Wi-Fi, B is 50 ms RTT / 10 Mbit/s down / 1 Mbit/s up, and C is 100 ms / 2 Mbit/s. Separate cold browser/SW, warm SW with cold GPU, and resident GPU. Retain older-iPhone, midrange Android and hardware-desktop checks. Desktop software rendering cannot establish phone speed. Run the 20-minute mixed workload and obtain Sean's matched-ground-scale visual approval before considering rollout.

The following numerical acceptance gates are **unchanged decisions**, not measured Stage 2 achievements:

| Gate | Required result and stop threshold |
|---|---|
| Prepared, profile B after five-second dwell | At least 95% of cap-fitting rounds fully prepare; prepared p95 last-input-to-Tsharp at most 100 ms and median at most 50 ms, with zero required post-input fetch/decode/upload. Failure stops rollout. |
| Whole experience, same dwell workload | p95 at most 300 ms, including incomplete, over-cap, evicted, unknown-coordinate and context-lost rounds. Do not hide these in a separate successful-only denominator. |
| Immediate cold | Profile B p95 first-input-to-Tsharp at most 3 s and at least 50% improvement over paired baseline; profile C p95 no worse than baseline with immediate stable fallback and bounded retries. |
| Motion and stability | p95 frame interval at most 20 ms at 60 Hz, no upload-attributable frame above 50 ms, and no context loss during the 20-minute mixed run. Record thermal degradation. |
| Resources and traffic | Page textures at most 64 MiB, compressed LRU at most 8 MiB, existing SW cap 100 MiB, speculative transfer at most 3 MiB per round and zero with Save Data. Representative five-round median unused speculation at most 1 MiB; full-round imagery transfer at most 2x consistently measured paired baseline. |
| Quality and reuse | At least 99% of available intended full-view imagery at the final pose, fade finished, no regression over the following 500 ms. Backdate Tsharp to the qualifying frame. Central 50% is diagnostic only. Native where available, declared fallback only where intended; ocean terminal base within one rendered frame when ready. No seams, color changes, known-absence requests or z10 dependency. Repeated resident deep zoom needs zero imagery fetch/decode/upload; one-tile pan retains every overlapping resident page. |

If any mandatory gate remains failed, do not ship. If the smaller-page pilot fails specifically on transfer, overfetch, draw overhead or upload cost, evaluate persistent final-z9 tiles at identical coverage and limits. Their **estimated** raw 70-tile RGBA8 mip payload at 256 square is about 23.33 MiB before gutters, context and driver costs, but they bring more requests and ownership objects and still need offline provenance resolution. They are a credible fallback, not a proven winner. If neither approach meets the gates, retain the current default and report the failed requirement.

The smaller measured JPEGs justify continuing a bounded investigation. They do not rescue the original residency design. The recommendation is **proceed with the stated changes**, with measured asset fit and fidelity closure before renderer construction and every existing phone latency and resource gate intact.
