# Pack hero photo provenance (traced 2026-11-01)

Method: the hero images are the card photos of three bank entries; their
image URLs are Wikimedia Commons thumb URLs, so each Commons filename was
queried through the Commons API (imageinfo extmetadata). No reverse image
search was needed. Records:

| Pack | Entry | Commons file | License | Author |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World Capitals | london-uk | London Skyline (125508655).jpeg | CC BY-SA 3.0 | Ilya Grigorik (via 500px, Archive Team import) |
| Landmarks | eiffel-tower | Eiffel tower from trocadero.jpg | CC BY-SA 3.0 | NonOmnisMoriar |
| Landforms | delicate-arch-utah | Delicate arch sunset.jpg | CC BY-SA 3.0 | Palacemusic |

Finding: all three are CC BY-SA 3.0. Commercial use is allowed, but the
pack art is a derivative (crop, foil treatment), which carries attribution
and share-alike obligations for the artwork. Options put to Sean
2026-11-01: keep them and add attribution (credits page already exists),
or swap the three heroes for public-domain / CC0 photographs of the same
subjects so the key art carries no encumbrance. The same three photos also
appear as ordinary postcard photos in game; the rest of the bank is
Commons-sourced the same way and carries no recorded license fields, which
is a wider hygiene item beyond these heroes.
