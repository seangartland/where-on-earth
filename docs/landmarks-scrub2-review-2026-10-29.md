## Where round 2 disagrees with round 1

Round 1 reviewed the whole older bank and allowed internationally famous venues, districts, routes, and themed outposts more latitude. This pass only covers the current 280-entry pack pool and applies Sean's stricter calibration: specialist functional venues do not qualify merely because their event is famous; a broad region or route must not masquerade as a point; and set membership does not rescue working stations or lesser-known settlements.

There are **71** changed verdicts among these pool IDs: 51 prior KEEP entries were downgraded, while 20 prior BORDERLINE entries became DROP. The clearest reversals are Sean's own examples—Daytona International Speedway, Amalfi Coast, and Vlkolínec—plus active Antarctic stations and long linear or regional subjects. Entries left BORDERLINE have a credible landmark case but still fail either broad-recognition or point-suitability cleanly enough that Sean should make the final call.
