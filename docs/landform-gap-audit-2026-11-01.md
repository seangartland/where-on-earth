## Methods

### Counts from this run

- Raw candidate titles: 33,130 from landmark-side sources and 33,393 from landform-side sources (61,415 distinct), then 12,056 further canonical articles from the supplement.
- Resolution (redirects followed, disambiguation pages dropped): 51,729 canonical articles from the main pass (2,168 titles missing, 13 disambiguation pages), plus the supplement's 12,056.
- Place filter: an article counts as a place only if it has coordinates. 34,441 main-pass articles had GeoData primary coordinates; Wikidata P625 restored coordinates for 704 more (of 5,058 checked, for example Mount Kilimanjaro, Galata Tower and the Hollywood Walk of Fame have no GeoData pin), and 3,022 supplement articles had coordinates.
- Matched to the covered set: 489 candidate articles (470 by canonical article, 19 by normalized name within 25 km of the card) covering 482 distinct bank or quarantine ids. A proximity guard revoked 27 name-only matches that pointed at a same-named place elsewhere (for example the Paris Panthéon had matched pantheon-rome); Badlands National Park was restored as an exact-title match.
- Measured with the REST API: 7,315 unmatched articles. 3,777 had a median of 300 or more and 1,234 of 1,000 or more before any classification.
- Screen: Wikidata instance-of types plus the short description removed 2,657 of the 300-plus rows as countries, cities, administrative divisions, events, ships, organizations and similar; 1,119 rows went to hand review, and 86 wrongly screened places (for example Blenheim Palace, Carthage, Sicily, Bonaire, Everglades) were rescued by reading the excluded list. Hand review dropped 338 kept rows that are not places or fail the bar outright (artworks, transit systems, stadiums and arenas, casinos, research sites, battles and other events, off-Earth features, fashion houses).
- Classified across both reports: 867 articles.

### API details

- Fame metric: Wikimedia REST pageviews, `/metrics/pageviews/per-article/en.wikipedia/all-access/user/<title>/daily/2025110100/2026103100`, the median of 365 daily values with missing days counted as zero. The dataset's own cut line is 100 median daily views; this audit's floors are 300 (notable) and 1,000 (glaring).
- Prefilter: the MediaWiki action API `prop=pageviews` (last 60 days) in batches of 50; only articles with a 60-day median of 150 or more, or with no 60-day data, were measured exactly. 542 REST requests that hit throttling on the first pass were retried until every row had a value.
- The exact article title used is the title shown in every table; each was resolved through redirects, and disambiguation pages were dropped before measuring. Coordinates and the nearest existing card (great-circle distance) were recorded for every row and used for the near-duplicate review.
- Matching normalization: lowercase, diacritics stripped, flag emoji and punctuation removed, a leading "the" dropped. Covered names come from each card's id, short and clue (full clue and the part before the first comma) across 635 bank and quarantine records, plus each card's canonical enwiki article resolved from those names (576 articles).
- Tokyo Tower is being added by a separate approved lane and was treated as covered. For reference it measured 585 median daily views on the same window.
- Model and method: Claude Opus 5.5, scripted pipeline in `.build-tmp-gap/` (not committed), hand classification of every 300-plus row. Pageview figures are measured; the one-line descriptions and the borderline reasons are judgment.
- Prior art: docs/famous-landforms-gap-2026-10-29.md is an older fame-first canon written against a 403-record bank. This audit re-measured from scratch and supersedes its missing list.
