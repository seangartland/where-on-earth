# Astra decision: proceed with the page production build

Decision date: 2026-11-22. Authority: Sean's decision assignment recorded in `BRIEF-astra-decision.md`. Evidence: that brief and `docs/zoom-stage2-evidence/burst-diagnosis.md`. This memo decides the implementation path; it does not authorize removal of the pilot flag today.

## Path and rationale

Choose **(i): proceed with the page design to a production build**, retaining `?pages=1` until the acceptance gates below pass. Do not begin the direct final-z9 persistent GPU tile fallback, and do not make source repair a prerequisite to starting renderer implementation. Keep that named fallback available until page acceptance is complete.

The phone trace demonstrates the intended mechanism: Madrid and Congo both used pages, with 24 and 22 textures respectively. Madrid's prepare took 969 ms and finished 1,895 ms before first input; Congo's prepare took about 500 ms. Madrid then had approximately 25 seconds of visibility-only interaction. The 66 Congo spans are exactly 22 page assets times three loading phases, wholly contained in prepare. Every tile-type span in the trace has `pool: 'pages'`; real legacy tile-pipeline spans total zero. The repeated Congo pin adds no loads. This supports pages, not a renderer replacement.

The apparent failure combines a page/legacy span naming collision with a report that compares later rounds against Madrid's first input. It is not evidence of eviction, duplicate satellite fetching, or interaction-triggered page loading. Two distinct production risks remain to address: unawaited preparation can overlap early input, and the ungated inset driver could run alongside pages when the camera drifts with satellite imagery live. The latter is an unconfirmed code-path risk, not an observed failure in this trace.

Source seams are independent of renderer choice. Spain's seasonal band and tile-aligned fill patches are present in the mosaic; changing renderers cannot correct them. Storage also does not force a fallback: the measured bucket occupies 3,265,874,688 bytes, leaving approximately 6.73 GB under the stated 10 GB allowance, with EOX retained.

## Order and scope

1. **Land both burst diagnostic fixes first. They are a gate for acceptance measurement, not a gate for starting the page build.** Rename page-store spans to `page-fetch`, `page-decode`, and `page-upload`, update consumers, and compute first input independently within each interval beginning at a `page-round` mark and ending at the next. Do not defer either fix. These changes are diagnostic-only; no cache, residency, or loading-policy change is justified by the alleged burst. Renderer implementation and source rebuilding may proceed in parallel while this patch lands, but no release verdict may rely on the old report.
2. **Build the production page lifecycle behind the existing flag.** Establish exclusive ownership of imagery loading during page preparation and active page rounds, suppressing new legacy inset requests in those states and resolving prior in-flight ownership before interactive readiness. Admit input for a page round only after its required assets and meshes are resident and ready. Show a preparing state during that interval. On preparation failure or unsupported coverage, make an explicit round-boundary transition to the existing supported path; do not activate late page results after that transition or after a newer round begins. Preserve existing behavior when the flag is absent. This readiness rule is the chosen response to fast input; normal clue dwell is not a correctness guarantee.
3. **Run bounded source-side seam repair in parallel with the page build, then regenerate its dependent assets before final acceptance.** The seven reported z5 keys are `5/3/2`, `5/3/3`, `5/1/2`, `5/1/3`, `5/4/4`, `5/4/3`, and `5/4/1`. Treat all seven as unresolved for historical source attribution. Neither inventories nor geometry/statistics QA can establish which scene or quarter supplied them. Do not spend another attribution pass trying to recover records that do not exist.

For repair, use the seven keys as geographic locators, not as proof of source selection. Form connected affected geographic windows from their bounds, including a one-z5-tile neighbor halo for boundary comparison and compositing context; clip or wrap at world boundaries. Include the photographed Spain band and fill-patch extents in those windows. If a visible discontinuity reaches the outer boundary, extend that window by one tile ring until the full defect and a clean comparison boundary are covered. Record the final bounds and why each extension was needed. This is a bounded reconstruction of affected windows, not an automatic global rebuild.

Reacquire or select available source imagery for those windows, making coherent quarter/scene and fallback selections across the defect rather than independently patching rendered tiles. Rebuild source mosaics with provenance recorded this time: source product/scene/granule identifiers as applicable, quarter/date, source path and checksum, selection/fallback decisions, footprint or contribution mask, tone/alignment parameters, build version, and output checksums. Explicitly record ocean/no-data fills. Every rebuilt output must map back to its contributing inputs; do not fabricate historical provenance for unchanged tiles.

Regenerate every intersecting shipped derivative from repaired source windows: the affected base/lower-zoom representations, live inset levels [5,7,8,9], and page assets. Include filter/compositing support at boundaries so stale neighboring derivatives cannot retain the seam. Stage a versioned candidate with a manifest connecting its outputs to the new provenance. Validate repaired interiors and joins against unchanged neighbors before release. Source repair is a gate for final visual acceptance and flag removal, not for starting renderer development.

4. **Integrate the repaired candidate with the production pages build, run the gates, and obtain Sean's final-map validation.** Only then lift the pilot flag and retire the unbuilt fallback option. EOX deletion remains a separate action and is not authorized by this memo.

## Measurable acceptance gates

The following are release criteria selected by this decision, not claims that the existing trace already satisfies them. Store the raw traces, report output, source manifest, visual comparisons, device details, and pass/fail results together. All gates must pass on the same candidate source and renderer versions.

### Correct diagnostic accounting

- Regression fixtures cover at least two rounds, a no-input round, a warm repeat, and preparation overlapping first input. New and historical page span names are classified explicitly by pool/type; legacy tile spans remain a separate count. A round without input reports that fact, not an inherited timestamp or an automatic overlap pass.
- Replaying the supplied historical trace must account for Madrid's 72 page phase spans, first Congo's 66, and repeated Congo's zero, with zero non-pages tile phase spans. Later preparation must not be labeled post-input merely because Madrid was already played.
- Report overlap from span intervals, not just start times: a load started before input but finishing after it counts as overlap. Assign spans to their originating round/preparation, including spans crossing a later round boundary; do not silently attribute their completion to the new round.

### Page lifecycle, responsiveness, and resource bounds

- On Sean's phone, run at least 20 page rounds: 10 with cold page caches and 10 warm, covering Madrid, Congo, repaired seam windows, coverage boundaries, and the largest admitted page plans. Also run 10 successive round transitions to expose accumulation. Preserve the tested network conditions in the evidence.
- For every admitted page round, required assets and meshes must be ready before gameplay input is enabled. Attempt input immediately during preparation, including under a recorded 1 Mbps / 150 ms RTT throttle and a forced asset failure. There must be zero accepted page-round gameplay inputs before readiness, zero stale page activations, and zero late page activations after fallback. Failure must reach an explicit recoverable state rather than leave the game silently stalled.
- For each ready round, exercise at least 30 seconds of zoom and camera pan/drift across the supported view range with the satellite layer live. Required result: zero page fetch/decode/upload spans and zero legacy inset fetch/decode/upload spans during that interactive interval. Warm repeats of a still-resident identical plan must add zero page asset loads. Round-start loading remains permitted and separately reported.
- In ordinary unthrottled cold runs on Sean's phone, preparation p95 must be at most 1,500 ms; warm preparation p95 at most 250 ms. During the 30-second interaction samples, frame interval p95 must be at most 33.4 ms, with zero renderer-attributable stalls over 100 ms and zero WebGL context losses. Use per-frame timing to measure this; the supplied trace's loading counts cannot establish frame performance.
- Every admitted plan must stay within the implementation's `PAGE_TEXTURE_CAP` and `PAGE_TRANSFER_CAP_BYTES`; record their numeric values and observed maxima in the acceptance artifact. Count total live page resources, not only the current plan. After each settled transition, resources must return within the documented resident budget, with no retained superseded meshes/textures outside that budget. Over-cap plans must take the explicit supported path without partially entering pages.
- Missing assets, unsupported coverage, and rapid round replacement must each pass an exercised case. Flag-off smoke coverage must preserve round start, guessing, zoom, and pan behavior. Keep the s2gm2025 inset ladder exactly [5,7,8,9]; z6 remains out of rotation and its stored objects remain untouched.

### Source quality and final approval

- All seven flagged z5 locations and the screenshot defect extents must be represented in the repair manifest. Every rebuilt asset must have a complete new provenance chain, with zero unexplained source/fallback selections and zero missing dependent outputs.
- Produce fixed-view before/after comparisons at wide zoom, z5, and final detail, plus comparisons across all repaired/unchanged joins. Acceptance requires zero visible seasonal bands or tile-aligned fill discontinuities at the flagged defects and zero newly introduced visible seams at repair boundaries. Use source selection/mosaic construction to achieve this; no blur or rendered-output concealment is permitted.
- Sean must explicitly validate the candidate map as final, including these comparisons and an on-phone check. Automated statistics, this memo, and the previously successful pilot trace cannot substitute for that approval.
- Before publishing candidate assets, measure projected retained storage including EOX, all existing prefixes, candidate outputs, and rollback assets. It must remain below 10,000,000,000 bytes. The existing 6.73 GB headroom is an estimate of available space, not permission to delete legacy data or assume unlimited replication.

The flag stays on until every gate passes and Sean's validation is recorded. Until then the default game remains unchanged. Abandon the direct final-z9 persistent GPU tile fallback only when these page gates pass; it is not abandoned merely because production implementation starts. A failure of source quality or diagnostic accounting does not justify switching renderers. If pages cannot satisfy lifecycle, frame-time, or resource gates after a targeted correction and rerun of the failing case, retain the flag and activate the named direct final-z9 persistent GPU tile fallback as the next implementation path, subject to the same source-quality, readiness, resource, and final-validation requirements. Do not run two renderer builds speculatively.

EOX stays in the bucket throughout implementation and acceptance. Even after Sean validates the final map, deletion requires a separate explicit decision; passing these gates does not itself schedule deletion.

## First implementation step

Create one diagnostic patch touching `src/globe/page-store.js`, `scripts/sat/page-trace-report.mjs`, and any discovered consumers of page-pool old span names. First search consumers of `tile-fetch`, `decode`, and `upload-submit` that select `pool: 'pages'`. Rename the three page-store span pairs, retain explicit historical pool-aware parsing in the report, and replace global first-input accounting with per-`page-round` interval accounting and span-overlap detection. Add the multi-round/no-input/warm-repeat/overlap fixtures above. Verify the historical counts 72/66/0 and zero legacy spans when the raw trace is available; if the raw trace is absent, retain that exact replay as a pending acceptance check rather than claim it ran. Land both fixes together before collecting acceptance traces. This is the first implementation patch, not an instruction to modify renderer residency to suppress legitimate preparation.
