# EOX source inventory report

Compiled 2026-10-18 by the orchestrator from the completed inventory files on disk. This report was written directly from the data files below, not generated by `scripts/sat/inventory.mjs`: the fix4 patch to that script assumed the low-resolution family shares the 10 m grid, which is wrong (see the low-resolution section), so its generator was not used and its patch was not banked.

Sources, labelled per family:

- 10 m family (`S2MSI_L3__MCQ`): CDSE OData catalogue, paged and reconciled by `scripts/sat/inventory.mjs`. Records: `scripts/sat/work/inventory/source-inventory.jsonl` (retained) and `scripts/sat/work/inventory/exclusions.jsonl` (excluded), with per-quarter catalogue counts in `scripts/sat/work/inventory/state.json`.
- Low-resolution family (`S2MSI_L3__MCQ_LR`): direct boto3 S3 census of the `eodata` bucket by the orchestrator, 2026-10-18. Record: `scripts/sat/work/inventory/lr-census.json`. This family appears zero times in the OData Products catalogue, so the census is its inventory of record.

All byte totals below use decimal GB (1 GB = 1e9 bytes), summed from the `contentLength` field of each record.

## Low-resolution family (source: boto3 S3 census)

The LR family uses its own lat/lon block grid: 36-degree blocks named by their southwest corner (for example `N18E000`), 50 blocks per quarter, 5 files per block (B02, B03, B04, B08, observations). It does not use the 10 m MGRS grid, so no footprint or cell can be borrowed from the 10 m catalogue. `scripts/sat/make-lr-inventory.mjs` builds the per-file LR inventory from the S3 listing (`scripts/sat/list-lr.py`) on this grid.

| 2025-Q2 | 50 | 250 | 15.55 GB |
| 2025-Q3 | 50 | 250 | 16.06 GB |
| 2025-Q4 | 50 | 250 | 17.37 GB |
| **Total** | **200** | **1,000** | **67.33 GB** |

Acquisition skips B08 in both families (Sean, 2026-10-18; rendering uses B02, B03, B04 plus observations). On a sampled LR block B08 was about 175 MB of 712 MB, so the skip saves roughly a quarter of the LR download: the base (LR) acquisition is estimated at about 51 GB against the 52 GB gate in the runbook. That figure is an estimate from the sampled block, not a census of B08 alone; the exact B08-free total lands when `make-lr-inventory.mjs` writes `lr-inventory.jsonl` from the listing.
