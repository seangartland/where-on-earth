This embeds the actual game (index.html?maplab=1) and swaps the river/lake outline layers on the live 3D globe, so what you see is exactly what would ship. Rivers come from Natural Earth 10m at full resolution; the lab shares the production asset (rivers-10m-scalerank.topo.json), which is always loaded and never affects normal gameplay. Lakes are the top N by size from the existing lakes-10m asset (531 total), with the largest 20 drawn bright and the rest dim. The ?maplab hook in src/main.js is inert unless the query param is present.
